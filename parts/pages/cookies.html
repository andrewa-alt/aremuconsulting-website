Cookie Policy

Which cookies and browser storage this site uses — and the fact it currently sets no non-essential or advertising cookies.

Cookies

Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. For clarity, we treat similar browser storage, such as local storage, in the same way in this policy.

Categories of cookies

  • Strictly necessary — essential for the site to work (for example, basic navigation and security). These do not require consent under PECR.
  • Functional — remember choices you make to improve your experience (for example, layout or language preferences).
  • Analytics — measure how the site is used (for example, page views and traffic sources).
  • Advertising — track you across sites to show targeted advertising.

What this site currently uses

This website sets no non-essential cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking, and it embeds no third-party content. The only storage involved is what your browser needs to display the site correctly (strictly necessary), and any self-hosted fonts are served from our own site.

Because we set no non-essential cookies, we are not currently required to show a cookie-consent banner.

If this changes

If we ever introduce analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy, describe what is set and why, and obtain consent where the law requires it before those cookies are placed.
