How it works

Inquiry → qualification → fit call. Send the intake below. Segun reviews and qualifies every inquiry first, replying within one working day to confirm whether it's worth a call. If it is, we book a free 15-minute fit call with Andrew — no charge, no obligation, and no scope work.

If the fit call goes well, the next step is the scoping consultation: a working session that produces a defined problem statement, a proposed architecture direction, a scope and milestone sketch, and a pricing shape. That is the product you are buying first; everything after it is a decision you make with that information in writing.

The scoping consultation is a fixed-scope discovery/audit: £500-£2,500 depending on depth, credited back against any signed build of £3,000+.

After handover (optional): care plans and pass-through running costs, described at handover and never sold as a squeeze in the consultation.
