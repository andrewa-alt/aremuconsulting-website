// case study — Accident Claims Intelligent Automator

Problem

A claims-management firm was losing the “golden hour”. Accident reports arrived as unstructured text, and by the time a person read and re-keyed each one, a competing insurer had already reached the claimant.

Architecture

A 24/7 automation reads every new report, runs liability triage against the firm’s criteria, and drafts the right first response for non-fault, at-fault, and third-party claimants. Registration numbers, accident notes, and contact details then sync into Zoho CRM automatically.

Trade-off

Anything with write access is model-drafted but routed through human review before it is sent. The guardrail costs a few minutes but keeps a person accountable for every message that leaves the firm.

Outcome

Reports are triaged in seconds rather than hours, the team works only the files that pass triage, and every record lands in the CRM complete — no manual re-keying.
